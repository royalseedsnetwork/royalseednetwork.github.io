Who We Are

A community for the priestly seeds.

Royal Seeds Network (RSN) is a community for pastors' and ministers' children, created to provide a safe and authentic space where they can connect with others who understand their journey, grow spiritually, heal from unique emotional and relational experiences, and discover who God has called them to become.

We believe that being born into a ministry family is more than a background. It is an opportunity to understand one's identity, develop a personal relationship with God, discover purpose, and prepare to make a meaningful contribution to the Kingdom and to society.

We don't simply want to tell priestly seeds who they should be. We want to help them understand who they are, whose they are, why they are here, and what they can become.
Our Four Pillars

Four dimensions of the journey.

01

Connecting

Creating opportunities for genuine relationships, community and belonging among pastors' and ministers' children — beyond titles, denominations and ministry backgrounds.

02

Growing

Encouraging spiritual growth and a personal relationship with Christ, so faith becomes personally owned rather than merely inherited.

03

Healing

Creating safe space for honest conversations around identity, expectations, relationships, family, emotional wellbeing, boundaries and the realities of growing up in ministry.

04

Discovering Purpose

Helping priestly seeds discover their gifts, passions and assignments — whether in ministry, business, government, diplomacy, technology, medicine, the arts or other fields.

Beyond the Four Walls

The Kingdom is bigger than the church building.

We believe priestly seeds are called not only to serve within Christian spaces but also to carry their identity, values, competence and influence into the wider world.

The goal is not simply to have Christians present in these spaces. It is to raise people who are equipped, excellent, principled and intentional enough to make meaningful impact while remaining authentic representatives of Christ.

Our Vision

A generation that knows who they are and why they are here.

To raise a generation of whole, grounded and purpose-driven priestly seeds who know Christ personally, understand their identity, embrace their unique assignments, and positively influence their generation.

Our Mission

To build a community that connects pastors' and ministers' children, nurtures their spiritual and emotional growth, provides safe spaces for healing and honest conversation, and equips them to discover and pursue their God-given purpose — within the church and beyond it.

What We Hope to See

Young people who know God for themselves, know who they are, are whole, understand their purpose, develop excellence, enter rooms of influence, represent Christ and build things that make their generation better.
